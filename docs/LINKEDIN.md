## Main version

> Two weeks ago I couldn't have told you what Decree 43 of 2013 says. This week
> I built an agent around it.
>
> I'm taking part in the Agentic AI Demo Challenge by @Decoding Data Science,
> and I built the **Dubai Tenancy Contract Auditor**.
>
> The problem is one almost everyone here has lived through. Dubai law caps rent
> increases — a sliding scale from 0% to 20%, depending on how far your rent
> sits below the market average. It's public, it's precise, and almost nobody
> reads it. So tenants pay whatever they're asked.
>
> Upload your tenancy contract and thirty seconds later you get three things:
> the legal maximum increase, the clauses in your contract that conflict with
> Dubai tenancy law, and the words to actually use with your landlord.
>
> The part I'm most pleased with isn't the model. It's that the agent
> *structurally cannot invent a law*. It cites each provision by ID, and the
> tool resolves that ID against statute text taken verbatim from the Dubai
> Legislation Portal — an ID that doesn't exist is rejected outright. Every
> figure in a verdict comes from a tested Python engine rather than the model.
> 103 tests cover it.
>
> And when it can't determine something — an area it has no data for, a scan it
> can't read — it says so instead of guessing. That was a deliberate choice: a
> confident wrong answer about someone's housing costs them money they never
> owed.
>
> 🔗 Live: https://dubai-tenancy-auditor.streamlit.app
> 💻 Code: https://github.com/som3a-web/dubai-tenancy-auditor
>
> Thanks @Decoding Data Science and @DDS Business Circle for running this.
>
> #AgenticAI #Dubai #AI #PropTech #BuildInPublic
